05Questions

Before you call us

Answers to what people ask most at the first visit. Anything else, the studio is happy to talk it through.

HL

Hannah Lowe, studio managerCall 012345678 or send a message.

How much does a new garden cost?

Most of our full design and build projects land between Ksh 2,340,000 and Ksh 8,450,000 depending on size, levels and how much hard landscaping is involved. Planting-only schemes start from about Ksh 6,800. You get a fixed, itemised quote before any work starts.

Can I have a design now and build it later?

Yes. Many clients build in phases, often hard landscaping one year and planting the next. The plan is drawn so each phase works on its own, and you are free to use any contractor to build it.

When is the best time to start?

Design can happen any time of year. We build hard landscaping from February to November and plant mostly in spring and autumn, when roots establish fastest. Spring build slots fill by late winter, so autumn is a good time to start a design.

Do you guarantee the plants?

Every plant we supply and plant is guaranteed for three years, as long as the watering guidance in your aftercare pack is followed. Patio and wall workmanship is guaranteed for ten years.

Are your lawn treatments safe for children and pets?

We use slow-release granular feeds and spot-treat weeds rather than blanket spraying. Most visits need nothing more than keeping pets off until the lawn is dry, and the visit note tells you if a treatment needs longer.

Which areas do you cover?

Nairobi, Willowmere Linden Rise, Hollins Cross, Westford and the rest of the Wyndham Valley, roughly 18 miles around the studio. See the contact page for the full list.
