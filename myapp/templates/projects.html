Garden design

The Yew Walk

Linden Rise

A long, narrow plot reworked as a gravel walk between clipped yews, ending at a hidden bench.

The owners wanted the length of the garden to feel like a journey. We set out a gently curving self-binding gravel path, planted twelve Irish yews as a rhythm along it and underplanted with ferns, hellebores and white foxgloves for the shade.

  • Self-binding gravel path with steel edging
  • 12 Irish yews and a shade border
  • Oak bench on a reclaimed brick pad
9 weeks · $31,800

Millbrook

A wildlife pond with natural stone edges, a timber pavilion and moisture-loving planting.

A wet corner of the garden that never drained became its best feature. We lined and shaped a pond with a shallow beach for wildlife, edged it in local stone and built a small timber pavilion facing west for evening light.

  • Clay-lined pond with a wildlife beach
  • Hand-set local stone edging
  • Cedar pavilion on a stone plinth
11 weeks · $58,200

Pollinator border

Nairobi

Forty yards of agastache, salvia, scabious and grasses that flower from June to the first frosts.

A sunny boundary that was mown grass is now a long border planted for bees. The scheme repeats six key plants in drifts so it reads well from the house and needs one cut-back a year.

  • 1,100 plants in drifts of six key species
  • Peat-free compost and gravel mulch
  • Annual cut-back on a maintenance plan
2 weeks · $9,400

Fieldstone terrace

Hollins Cross

A dry-laid fieldstone wall levels a sloping lawn into two terraces under mature pines.

The lawn sloped so steeply it was hard to mow or sit on. We built a 30-yard dry-stone retaining wall from stone found on site, levelled two lawn terraces and returfed them, with a flight of stone steps between.

  • Dry-stone wall from reclaimed site stone
  • Two levelled, returfed lawn terraces
  • Stone steps with low-voltage lighting
6 weeks · $27,300
Planting

Cottage front garden

Old Mill Lane

Roses, daisies and herbs planted tight against an old stone cottage, with room left for self-seeders.

A front garden of gravel and a single shrub became a full cottage planting. We improved the thin soil, planted old shrub roses, leucanthemum, nepeta and herbs, and left gaps for poppies and forget-me-nots to seed into.

  • Old shrub roses and long-flowering perennials

Orchard Lane

The sunniest strip of the garden is now a productive kitchen garden. Raised larch beds at sitting height, a drip irrigation line on a timer and a cold frame mean the family can grow salads and beans with twenty minutes’ work a day.

Lawn care

Lawn renovation

Beech Park

A tired, mossy lawn scarified, aerated and overseeded, then kept on a Complete care plan.

The lawn under these old beeches was more moss than grass. We scarified hard, hollow-tine aerated, top-dressed and overseeded with a shade mix, then moved it onto a nine-visit plan. A year on it is thick and even.

  • Scarifying, hollow-tine aeration and top-dressing
  • Overseeded with a shade-tolerant mix
  • Nine-visit Complete lawn care plan
3 days · $2,450
