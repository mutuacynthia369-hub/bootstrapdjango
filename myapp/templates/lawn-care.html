04Between visits

Three habits that do the most

Feeding does half the work. The other half is how the lawn is cut and watered between our visits.

Mow high, mow often

Keep the blades at 2.5 to 3 inches and never take more than a third off in one cut. Taller grass shades out weeds and holds moisture.

Water deeply, not daily

One long soak a week in dry spells, early in the morning, trains roots to go deep. Little and often keeps them at the surface.

Leave the clippings

Short clippings left on the lawn break down in days and return nitrogen to the soil, so the lawn needs less feed.
